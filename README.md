# Groceries

Open the `App.js` file to start writing some code. You can preview the changes directly on your phone or tablet by scanning the **QR code** or use the iOS or Android emulators. When you're done, click **Save** and share the link!

## Objectives

- Your groceries app must contain at least two tabs
  - The first tab should list all the available products
  - The second tab should contain the products you've added to your basket (incl. quantity, price, etc)
    - The tab icon/title should show the basket total
    - There should be a button at the bottom, which allows you to checkout once your total is R5.00
- Once your basket has hit R10.00, show the user a popup/toast notification to make the user aware of free delivery
- You have free range on the styling of your groceries app
  - Take advantage of your favourite styling tool(s) and let the creativity flow
  - Add icons
  - etc
- Use a global state management library of your choosing
- Add unit tests were applicable
- It must work on both iOS and Android
  - [Optional] Support web
- [Optional] This is also an opportunity to show us something cool
